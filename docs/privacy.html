Voice Camera: Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Voice Camera” (package com.plotfarms.voicecamera)

In short:

Voice Camera has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Your videos and photos stay on your phone. The app never sends them anywhere on its own. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you tap Upload and choose another app to share with.

Who we are

Voice Camera is published by Plotfarms (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy: info@plotfarms.com

What the app can access, and why

PermissionWhy the app needs it
CameraTo show the viewfinder and to record videos and take photos when you ask.
MicrophoneTo record sound with your videos, and to listen for your voice commands (for example “record video after 5 seconds”).

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, calendar, phone, call log, messages or files. It saves your recordings with Android’s normal media storage, so no storage permission is needed.

Videos and photos

  • Videos are saved to Movies/VoiceCamera and photos to Pictures/VoiceCamera on your phone, where your Gallery app can show them.
  • We do not receive, copy, upload or look at them. The app has no server and no internet permission, so it cannot send them.
  • The Trim feature works on your phone and saves the trimmed video as a new file next to the original.
  • You can delete any of them inside the app or from your Gallery at any time. Deleting is up to you; the app keeps no other copy.

Voice commands

Voice commands use Android’s built-in speech recognition service, which is provided by your phone’s manufacturer or Google and is part of your phone, not of this app. While voice control is on, the app asks that service to listen and give back the words it heard. Depending on your phone and settings, that service may process your voice on the device or send it to its provider’s servers. That processing is governed by the provider’s own privacy policy (for Google, see policies.google.com/privacy).

Voice Camera itself does not record or keep your voice for this purpose, does not store what you said, and does not send it anywhere. It uses the recognised words only to understand a command, and shows them briefly on the screen. Voice control only listens while the camera screen is open. It pauses while a video is recording, and stops when you leave the app. You can switch it off with the microphone icon at any time.

Information the app stores on your phone

The app keeps a few small settings on your phone: your default wait and recording length, and which of your takes you marked with a heart. This stays on the phone, is not sent to us, and is removed if you uninstall the app (your videos and photos are not removed by uninstalling).

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information, because we do not collect it. When you tap Upload the app opens Android’s share sheet. If you pick an app there (for example WhatsApp, Drive or YouTube), that video or photo is handed to that app and then handled under its privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Ads, analytics and tracking

None. The app has no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking code.

Children

The app is a general-purpose camera and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Security

Because your recordings stay on your phone, they are as safe as your phone is. Use a screen lock, and be careful which apps you share them with.

Your choices

  • Turn voice control off with the microphone icon, or take away the Camera or Microphone permission in Android Settings > Apps > Voice Camera > Permissions.
  • Delete any video or photo in the app or the Gallery.
  • Uninstall the app to remove its settings.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change ships.
